The Brass Key

Eleanor, an adult railway station keeper in a dark green wool coat, begins her final shift at a rural station scheduled to close. A storm has loosened the shutters. The office clock shows that the final service is due soon. Eleanor checks the platform lamp and lowers the shutters by hand.

Theo, an adult traveler in a tan raincoat carrying a canvas satchel, arrives before dusk. He is carrying a brass key tied to a red ribbon. It belonged to his mother, who once worked in the station. He wants to return it before the station closes. Theo says, "She asked me to bring this back before the last train."

Eleanor recognizes the ribbon but the key no longer opens the office. She recalls helping his mother repair the old platform lamp. She says, "It was never for the office. Come with me."

They cross the platform and find that the lamp's service panel is locked. Theo holds the rain off with his coat while Eleanor tests the key. The panel unlocks. Inside, a small folded note thanks Eleanor for keeping the light on during a winter evacuation. Eleanor finishes seating the loose lamp contact, closes the panel, and passes the note to Theo. She says, "Your mother left this for both of us."

The lamp illuminates as the final train approaches. Theo boards with the note. Eleanor places the brass key on the office hook, then watches the train leave in the rain. The final image shows the lit platform lamp outside the closed station.
